A LITTLE GUIDE

How to Play

Catch the wandering mice before they scurry off. Here is how each mode works.

Relax & Play

4 LIVES

You start with four lives. Every mouse that escapes costs one life. When you run out, your session ends.

  • Misses cost a life.
  • Build your score and combo by catching mice.
  • Difficulty gradually rises as your score grows.

Endless & Chill

NO LIVES LIMIT

Keep playing for as long as you like. Mice can escape without ending your run.

  • Missing a mouse resets your combo, not your run.
  • Try to beat your best score.
  • The game still gets busier as your score increases.

Pro Mode

3 LIVES · HARD

A faster, more demanding challenge for experienced mouse catchers.

  • Start with only three lives.
  • Mice move faster and appear more often.
  • Difficulty rises every 10 points, and the catch area is smaller.

Little tips

Regular mice are worth 1 point; golden mice are worth 5. Catching mice earns cheese treats for upgrades, and catching enough over time unlocks hats in the Mouse Shop.

Relax & Play and Endless & Chill raise difficulty every 15 points. Pro Mode raises it every 10 points, with faster spawns and a smaller catch area. From higher levels, extra mice may appear.
